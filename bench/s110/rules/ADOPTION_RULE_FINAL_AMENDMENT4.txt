LABEL: POST-HOC. It is written after S1-10R phase 3 (S1-7 stress with the tier on at P*) recorded swap, and before any
0.33.0 gate boot. It adds a record requirement and a statement of status; it changes no threshold of any criterion.

EVIDENCE (S1-10R phase 3, muse_wf/s1-10r/phase3_stress.json meta.swap_finding, docs/s110_numa_offload.md section 4a):
- At P* = 51,814,072,320 B per rank, 7 of the 9 valid ON boots swapped out 3.7-5.5 GiB of unpinned worker / EngineCore /
  API-server anonymous memory (process VmSwap 0.2-1.9 GiB per worker on all four GPU nodes). Boot-time swap occurred in 6 of
  the 10 boots (spec_2, ns256_1, ns128_1, ns256_2, ns128_2, ns128_3) and not in spec_1, ns256_3, ns256_3b, ns256_4; ns256_4
  swapped 3.7 GiB during the stress. The gap to the previous teardown does not separate the two groups: the cause is open.
  No OOM kill, no watchdog trip, 0 errors; the swapped pages were cold; READY 119-140 s with boot-time swap, 100-110 s without.
- The phase 3 pre-stated "no swap" check FAILED in all three confirming boots (phase 3 all_pass=false).
- No criterion of ADOPTION_RULE_FINAL (G1-G5, P1-P5, X, S, T, D) or of amendments 1-3 looks at swap. G3 requires
  min(MemFree + file cache) >= 4,096 MiB, READY <= median OFF READY + 180 s and 0 watchdog trips; G1 / G2 / P1-P5 measure the
  throughput and latency effects of everything the ON arm does, including swap-ins.

CHANGE:
 (1) SWAP RECORD, mandatory for EVERY boot of the phase 4 gate chain that has a server (ON, OFF and CTL boots, the D / D4'
     processes, gates S and T). The frozen drivers are not changed; the phase 4 driver copies (their md5s are recorded before
     the first gate boot) take the record into the boot directory (swap_<tag>.json), at three points: before the launch,
     at READY, and after the last job of the boot (before the teardown):
       - swap used = SwapTotal - SwapFree (/proc/meminfo, MiB) and /proc/vmstat pswpin and pswpout;
       - VmSwap (/proc/<pid>/status, kB) of every process of the launched session (the 8 workers, EngineCore, API server),
         at READY and at the end;
       - derived: pswpout delta launch -> READY, READY -> end, and the pswpin delta READY -> end.
     The report of the chain prints one swap table (per boot: swap used at READY and at the end, the deltas, the largest
     per-process VmSwap) next to the verdict. A boot whose record is missing is listed as MISSING; this changes no verdict.
 (2) STATUS: INFORMATIONAL. Swap is not a gate criterion: no outcome of G1-G5, P1-P5, X, S, T or D depends on it, no boot is
     invalidated or repeated because of swap, and P* (51,814,072,320 B per rank, env/offload.env) stays the tier size of every
     ON arm. Reasons: the harm of swap is latency, which G1, G2 and P1-P5 measure directly on the ON arm, and memory safety is
     covered by G3 (4,096 MiB floor, watchdog) and by the 0 OOM / 0 preemption guards.
 (3) CONSEQUENCES, stated now so they are not decided after the verdict: an ADOPT verdict is reported together with the swap
     table and is NOT described as swap-free; the production launch note keeps the swap observation of phase 3 and the
     preflight_offload.sh requirement. If a G1 / G2 / P criterion fails in an ON boot that shows a pswpin delta READY -> end > 0,
     the report names the swap-in as a candidate cause; that does not change the verdict. Reducing P* (for example by a few
     GiB, to separate "state left by the previous teardown" from "node pressure at P*") is NOT part of this gate; it would be a
     new tier size with its own S1-7 stress and its own amendment, written before the boots it governs.
UNCHANGED: every criterion, threshold, arm, session design and tool of ADOPTION_RULE_FINAL.txt and amendments 1-3.
